# Privacy Policy — Shipper Phố Cổ

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Shipper Phố Cổ ("the app") is made by Bão Bão ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles. Contact: ducbaonguyen101098@gmail.com.

## What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account, we run no servers of our own, and the app has no analytics or crash reporting of its own.

- **On your device:** your game progress, records, savings, upgrades, badges, daily challenges and settings. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this data in that backup and restore it when you reinstall the app.

## Third-party services

- **Google AdMob** shows ads in the Android app (optional rewarded ads and occasional ads between rounds). It may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate general location), ad and app interactions, and diagnostic information, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
- The web version of the game shows no ads and sends no data.

## Your choices

- In the European Economic Area, the UK and other regions where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice any time with "Quyền riêng tư" at the bottom of the main menu.
- On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

## How long data is kept and how to delete it

- Data on your device stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data (Android: Settings → Apps → Shipper Phố Cổ → Storage → Clear storage). Android backups are managed in your Google account settings.
- AdMob keeps data as described in Google's policy above.
- For any privacy request, email ducbaonguyen101098@gmail.com. Because the app has no accounts, we may ask for details that help us find the data.

## How data is protected

All data the app sends travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We do not store your data on our own servers.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page and update the effective date.
