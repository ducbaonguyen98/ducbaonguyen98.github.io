# Privacy Policy — Color Sort: Water Puzzle

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Color Sort: Water Puzzle ("the app") is made by Bão Bão ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles. Contact: ducbaonguyen101098@gmail.com.

## What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account, and we run no servers of our own.

- **On your device:** your game progress, settings and whether you bought "Remove Ads". If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this data in that backup and restore it when you reinstall the app.
- **Sent to Google Firebase Analytics:** how the game is played, such as levels started, finished, restarted or skipped, the number of moves and time taken, daily challenge streaks, rewarded-ad requests and results, and whether "Remove Ads" was bought or restored. Firebase also records app opens, sessions and in-app purchases automatically, together with an app instance identifier, your device model, operating system, app version and approximate country.
- **Sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics:** crash reports with technical details of the error, device model, operating system and app version.

## Third-party services

The app uses these services, which collect data under their own privacy policies:

- **Google AdMob** shows ads. It may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate general location), ad and app interactions, and diagnostic information, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
- **Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config)** helps us understand how the game is played, fix crashes and tune game settings, using the data listed above and a Firebase installation identifier. Firebase Analytics in this app does not collect your advertising ID and is not used for ad personalisation. Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- **Google Play and the Apple App Store** process the optional "Remove Ads" purchase. We never see your payment details; the store tells the app only whether the purchase was made.

## Your choices

- In the European Economic Area and the UK, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
- On iPhone, the app asks for permission before tracking. You can change it in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

## How long data is kept and how to delete it

- Data on your device stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data (Android: Settings → Apps → Color Sort → Storage → Clear storage). Android backups are managed in your Google account settings.
- Firebase Analytics keeps event data for up to 14 months. Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days. AdMob keeps data as described in Google's policy above.
- To ask us to delete data linked to your device, or for any other privacy request, email ducbaonguyen101098@gmail.com. Because the app has no accounts, we may ask for details that help us find the data.

## How data is protected

All data the app sends travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We do not store your data on our own servers; it is held by Google's services under their security practices, and our access to their consoles is protected by our Google account.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page and update the effective date.

Chúng tôi không yêu cầu tên, email hay tài khoản, và không có máy chủ riêng.

- **Gửi tới Google Firebase Analytics:** cách trò chơi được chơi, ví dụ màn đã bắt đầu, hoàn thành, chơi lại hay bỏ qua, số nước đi và thời gian, chuỗi ngày thử thách, yêu cầu và kết quả xem quảng cáo có thưởng, và việc mua hay khôi phục "Remove Ads". - **Gửi tới Google Firebase Crashlytics:** báo cáo lỗi kèm chi tiết kỹ thuật, kiểu thiết bị, hệ điều hành và phiên bản ứng dụng.

Firebase Analytics trong ứng dụng này không thu thập mã quảng cáo và không dùng để cá nhân hóa quảng cáo. Chính sách: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
