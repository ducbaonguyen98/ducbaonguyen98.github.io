Color Season Mirror — Support

How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Open Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy.

How do I remove ads?

Upgrade to Premium from the app. Premium removes all ads.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings → Language and pick one of the 15 available languages.
