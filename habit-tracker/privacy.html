Habit Tracker — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-01

This policy explains what information Habit Tracker ("the app") uses and why. The app has no user accounts and no servers of its own.

Data you create in the app

Everything you create in the app is stored on your device (and in your device backup, if you use iCloud or Google backup). We have no servers and cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it from the device.

Information collected by our partners

  • Google AdMob shows ads in the free version. It may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address (used to estimate an approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the EU/UK you are asked for consent first, and on iOS you are asked for tracking permission. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • RevenueCat processes in-app purchases. It receives your purchase history and an anonymous app user ID — never your name or payment details (Apple and Google handle payments). Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Data retention and deletion

We do not keep any of your data ourselves. Our partners keep the data listed above for as long as their own policies allow. To request deletion of data held by a partner, use the partner links above or email us and we will help you. You can withdraw ad consent at any time (Settings → Privacy choices) and turn off ad tracking in your device settings.

Security

Data sent to our partners is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). We only use partners that protect personal data under terms at least as strict as this policy.

Your choices

You can change your ad consent at any time in the app (Settings → Privacy choices, where available), limit ad tracking in your device settings, or upgrade to Premium to remove ads.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.
